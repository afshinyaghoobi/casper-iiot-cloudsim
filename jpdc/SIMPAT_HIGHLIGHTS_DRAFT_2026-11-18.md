**Title:** Queue-Wait and Execution-Time Semantics in CloudSimPlus 8.5.7: Implications for IoT Scheduling Simulation

- CloudSimPlus 8.5.7 queue waits are compared with an explicit FIFO reference.
- Twenty-four native cases cover 288 completed CPU-only simulation tasks.
- Native and FIFO wait gaps reach 246 ms in the tested synthetic cases.
- Retrospective execution durations leave a 154 ms maximum wait gap.
- Datacenter arrival and submit clocks match in all 288 tested tasks.

## Prospective state-machine definitions
Processing original rows in nondecreasing timestamp order; key exact pair `(job_id,task_index)`, maintain strictly observed **within-window** event history, not hypothetical earlier history:
- `FIRST_OBSERVED_SUBMIT_LEFT_CENSORED`: SUBMIT and no prior events for key within this window. **Not proof that this is first lifetime SUBMIT**.
- `RESUBMIT_AFTER_OBSERVED_TERMINAL`: SUBMIT with immediately preceding event for exact key among `EVICT,FAIL,FINISH,KILL,LOST`. Report separate preceding types and mark FINISH→SUBMIT anomalous/semantically ambiguous; do NOT silently assume legal retry.
- `SUBMIT_AFTER_OTHER_HISTORY`: SUBMIT after another within-window event (e.g. SCHEDULE, UPDATE or earlier SUBMIT), not safe to call initial.
- `MATCHED_OBSERVED_SUBMIT_TO_SCHEDULE`: SCHEDULE after the most recent within-window SUBMIT for same key, where there is no intervening SCHEDULE or terminal event. Record one matched scheduling gap per observed SUBMIT, minimum0, and classify by type of that SUBMIT (left-censored / postterminal / other). No match across window boundaries.
- `SCHEDULE_WITHOUT_OBSERVED_PENDING_SUBMIT`: SCHEDULE when no within-window open submit; **LEFT_CENSORED**, not zero wait or malformed. A terminal event with/without observed preceding SCHEDULE similarly recorded as observed/unpaired without declaring missing workload or conservation failure.
- `RIGHT_CENSORED_PENDING_SUBMIT`: SUBMIT still unmatched at end window; this includes pending/resubmitted work. Observed multi-entry task keys and source-job group sizes recorded to demonstrate dependence, not treated as IID tasks.

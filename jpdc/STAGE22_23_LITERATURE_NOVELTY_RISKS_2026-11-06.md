## Specific novelty question to settle before manuscript
What new algorithmic guarantee survives (a) missing or conditionally invalid network observations, (b) queue-prediction error and CloudSimPlus admission quirks, (c) hard mission deadlines, and (d) changing feedback delays **and is not a direct restatement of an established robust upper/lower bound dominance policy**? Stage22's simple lemma expressly requires q_j=0; the adversarial queue error counterexample invalidates any unconditional guarantee. Source-specific L=.0001/ms and 10000ms freshness are NOT data-calibrated. Do not rebrand old FGCS CASPER original as JPDC novelty.

## Mandatory experiments before strong submission claim
Match relevant published algorithms as closely as feasible, transparent implementations and hardware/queue assumptions, multiple independent paired sources and fault severities, separate validation of different queue policy / model, realistic external traces, formal complexity and robust evaluation. Stage16 adverse S2 / nonsignificant S3 and Stage17 n2 probe null must remain in the evidence ledger. Stage23 seed88 is DEVELOPMENT only regardless of observed results.

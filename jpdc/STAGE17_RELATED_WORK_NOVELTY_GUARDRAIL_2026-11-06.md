# JPDC Stage17 — targeted related-work novelty guardrail (2026-11-06)

This is a preliminary *verified bibliographic shortlist*, not a completed systematic review. The mere use of E2E-only delayed feedback, server-state staleness, proactive telemetry or deadlines is **not new**.

1. Y. Li et al., "Cloud-Edge System for Scheduling Unpredictable LLM Requests With Combinatorial Bandit," *IEEE Transactions on Services Computing* 18(6):3567–3580, 2025, DOI [10.1109/TSC.2025.3611379](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSC.2025.3611379). Combines cloud-edge online scheduling and **delayed feedback**, relevant to offloading and learning comparisons but distinct LLM workloads.
2. "Task Offloading and Scheduling Under Hard Deadlines in Vehicular Edge Computing Systems," *IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology* 74(6):10029–10034, 2025, DOI [10.1109/TVT.2025.3539885](https://doi.org/10.1109/TVT.2025.3539885). Online practical edge-server information acquisition under **hard deadlines**; a critical comparison to source-info-limited scheduling.
3. S. A. Hamid et al., "Exploiting and Mitigating Staleness in Distributed Edge Offloading via Predictive Load Awareness," *Network* 6(3):69, published 2026-08-28, DOI [10.3390/network6030069](https://doi.org/10.3390/network6030069). **Crucial challenge to simplistic freshness thesis:** staleness can also reduce synchronized migrations, hence naive fresh-information chasing can be detrimental; supports explicit load/congestion guard research direction, but does not establish novelty of our simple +2ms rule.
4. "Forecast-driven task offloading for reliable and adaptive mobile edge computing," *Computer Communications*, 2026, [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S108480452600069X). Integrates forecast + contextual bandit under nonstationary server load/failure; important competing scheduling baseline family, not currently implemented.
5. M. Pandey, Y.-W. Kwon, "Event-Driven Double Dueling Deep Q-Learning for Efficient Serverless Edge Function Offloading and Scheduling," *IEEE Transactions on Cloud Computing* 14(3):1802–1818, 2026, DOI [10.1109/TCC.2026.3703387](https://doi.org/10.1109/TCC.2026.3703387). Queue congestion, deadline and real Azure/Alibaba traces strengthen expectations for independent real-trace validation.

The manuscript's genuinely defensible potential remains **the explicit interaction of delayed *measurement-generation* time, conditional identifiability under uncertain drift, congestion-controlled feedback acquisition, and hard-deadline scheduling**, conditional on an original testable decision mechanism and stronger performance/fidelity evidence. An age bound from a stylized source-specific 2x shock, by itself, is not a novel externally justified statistical guarantee. Stage16 non-significant S3 and S2 harm remain binding negative evidence. No paper novelty claim is approved by this note.

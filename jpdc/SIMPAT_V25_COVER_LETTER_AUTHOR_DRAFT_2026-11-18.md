# Cover letter — SIMPAT submission draft (requires corresponding-author approval)

**To:** The Editor, *Simulation Modelling Practice and Theory* (Elsevier)

**Subject:** Manuscript submission — Queue-Wait and Execution-Time Semantics in CloudSimPlus 8.5.7: Implications for IoT Scheduling Simulation

Dear Editor,

We submit for consideration our manuscript entitled “Queue-Wait and Execution-Time Semantics in CloudSimPlus 8.5.7: Implications for IoT Scheduling Simulation.” The paper examines the interpretability of queue-wait and execution-time measurements in CloudSimPlus 8.5.7 SpaceShared when compared with a clearly specified, event-independent, capacity-matched FIFO reference. It is a simulation-methodology and reproducibility study, not a proposal for a new scheduling policy.

The empirical evidence includes a prespecified factorial of 24 synthetic cases and 288 completed CPU-only Cloudlets, together with source-level native event traces and control experiments. The maximum absolute native-versus-reference waiting discrepancy across cases is 246 ms, while model-based synthetic deadline classifications differ for 86 of 288 and 107 of 288 tasks at the two prespecified thresholds. The study also investigates the influence of retrospective execution-duration accounting, confirms clock-origin agreement within the fixed tested configuration, and reports a negative result for a separate input-only event-gap hypothesis. Original logs and analysis outputs are archived with source hashes and an executable provenance trail.

The submission is relevant to SIMPAT readers assessing the validity, reproducibility, and measurement assumptions of cloud/edge scheduling simulations. We have limited our conclusions to the tested synthetic single-VM configuration: the comparison does not establish a simulator defect or real-world IoT deadline error.

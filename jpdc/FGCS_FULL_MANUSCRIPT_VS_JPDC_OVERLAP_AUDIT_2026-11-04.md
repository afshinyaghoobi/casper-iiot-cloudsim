## Executive originality judgment: high risk, now supported by first-paper full text

**NO-GO for submission of the current JPDC text as an independent algorithm paper. High overlap risk even for the proposed systems/negative-results framing.** The actual FGCS manuscript already positions **uncertainty-certified vs realized deadlines**, *selected-node vs candidate-family coverage*, the special S3 network-degradation failure, *rank/queue counterfactual limits*, *health-vs-network feedback mismatch*, and *abstention and certificate tradeoffs* as central contributions or Discussion findings. Calling any of those the JPDC paper's new discovery without a materially different method or research question would duplicate the first paper's narrative.

The stronger JPDC-exclusive material is **controlled physical LOW/HIGH mask location intervention**, **explicit fixed-eta factorial counterfactual**, **ten-seed two-sided preregistered follow-up for a DIFFERENT primary question**, and **independent event-driven native Python reproduction with separately computed calibration and physical outcomes**. These are meaningful added evidence/methodology, but the full manuscript, venue fit, and sufficiently substantial novel theoretical/computational contribution remain unproven; new primary effect tests were nonsignificant. The native model is strict single-server FIFO and differs from CloudSimPlus SpaceShared; previously preregistered Java-to-Python physical queue wait fidelity FAIL remains decisive disclosure.

## Specific evidence showing the proposed Path A title overlaps more than previously thought

Previously proposed JPDC provisional title:
> *When Adaptive Certificates Do Not Improve Deadline Completion: Exposure-Aware Validation of Cloud–Edge Scheduling.*

FGCS **already states in §7 (p. 18)** that ETA is more closely associated with improved coverage and screening behavior than with detectable DVR improvements, describes how selected-node Mission feedback can fail to protect non-selected family nodes during hidden network degradation, and separates the operational screening flag from actual deadline outcomes. The same page discusses the absence of independent health/probe effect attribution and warns about one-step family counterfactual limitations.

**Revised gate:** This exact Path A title/central message has a **HIGH overlap risk** and should NOT be treated as a sufficiently distinct JPDC contribution without a fundamentally expanded **causal design, estimator, or new physical system**. The previous `JPDC_ONE_PAGE_CONTRIBUTION_CHARTER_2026-11-04.md` is superseded as a standalone recommended title by this evidence update; retain it as historical decision provenance, do not silently edit away its earlier proposal.

## What is actually incremental in the present JPDC work

1. **Instrumented, matched exact-eta contrast** under frozen mask: the original first manuscript describes component-level ablation but did not run a generated E45 effective-eta clamp against the health-static counterpart holding every other component fixed. This follow-up isolates certificate conservatism, yet the Java disjoint holdout **p=.0625** and n4 mask/eta **p=.125** are nonsignificant.
2. **Pre-shock placement-informed selected LOW/HIGH affected masks** with explicitly measured realized exposure, showing dramatic dependence of simulated risk and ETA on *fault location rather than nominal severity*. This is a meaningful experimental design contribution but not a new policy. The masks are extreme pre-change selections, not samples from a natural fault process; selected route placement is endogenous to source scenario.
3. **Native cross-language physical engine, complete all-eight mode, independent raw native calibration, 10 unseen seed primary comparison**: not available in FGCS submitted manuscript. However synthetic source model remains shared; native strict FIFO is not CloudSim equivalent and the primary experiment does not show superior deadline performance (two-sided exact sign p=1.0, 5 favorable, 4 unfavorable, 1 tie).
4. **Actual rank2 utilization and precise E17 event chronology diagnostics**, strengthening explanation but not deriving a new dispatch decision method.

The increment is primarily **experimental methodology and falsification of optimistic inferences**, not presently an original scheduling algorithm.

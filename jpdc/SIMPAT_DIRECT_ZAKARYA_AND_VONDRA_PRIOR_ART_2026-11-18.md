## Critical conclusion and source-use discipline

**Method novelty is still not demonstrated at SIMPAT full-research-article level.** New genuine measurements are fixed native Java A1/A2/A3 (SpaceShared8.5.7, 24 cases/288 jobs; source-defined wait and same-clock A3; FIFO comparator; posthoc duration sensitivity). The reference *deliberately differs* from native scheduling; the observed gap is not by default “simulation error”, proof of a faulty simulator, causal identification of specific update branches, or operational real IoT queue fidelity. No new model replacement, generally valid oracle, field trace or independent hardware benchmark is claimed. This journal's published precedents have stronger real workload/hardware validation.
